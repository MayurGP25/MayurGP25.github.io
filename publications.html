  • [01]
    Accepted · UncertaiNLP @ EMNLP 2026 Sole author

    Constrained Decoding Can Reverse Which Inputs Look Uncertain

    Mayur Parvatikar

    The 3rd Workshop on Uncertainty-Aware NLP (UncertaiNLP) at EMNLP 2026

    Grammar-constrained (XGrammar JSON) decoding reverses which inputs look uncertain: ranking AUROC moves from 0.13–0.18 to 0.61–0.75 while post-mask entropy still looks normal. The reversal tracks how surviving probability mass is distributed, making pre-mask entropy the reliable signal.

    PATH-DISSECT: Interpreting Concept Representation and Causal Reliance in Pathology Foundation Models with Sparse Autoencoders

    Mayur Parvatikar, Xingjian Li, Min Xu

    Mechanistic Interpretability for Medical Foundation Models (MI4MedFM) Workshop at MICCAI 2026, Strasbourg, France

    Sparse autoencoders trained on frozen UNI and Virchow2 embeddings recover pathologist-recognizable concepts; the framework separates what the model represents from what its classifier relies on, and mitigates shortcut reliance.

  • [03]
    Submitted · AAAI 2027 Second author

    Swiss-Knife: A Framework for Reconfigurable Externalised Multi-Objective Alignment at Decode Time

    A. Karmakar, Mayur Parvatikar, S. Dhoot, A. Dhanda, A. Chadha, K. Wanaskar, V. Jain, A. Das

    arXiv:2609.22226, 2026 · submitted to AAAI 2027

    Steers a frozen Qwen2.5-7B with hot-swappable helpful, honest and harmless DPO-LoRA adapters, switching objectives in 0.05 ms without retraining. Harmonic F1 0.797 vs. 0.750 over five decode-time baselines, with the lowest refusal rate and 47% lower toxicity.
