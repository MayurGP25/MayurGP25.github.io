    To be submitted · AAAI 2027 Third author

    MENTIS: What Belief Changes Under Alignment? Measuring Multi-Scale Latent Torsion in Language Models

    Partha Pratim Saha, Samarth Raina, Mayur Parvatikar, Amit Dhanda, Vinija Jain, Aman Chadha, Amitava Das

    To be submitted to AAAI 2027, AI Alignment Track

    Introduces a geometry-based framework that measures how preference alignment reorganizes a model’s internal representations, localizing where and how strongly alignment reshapes different concepts.
