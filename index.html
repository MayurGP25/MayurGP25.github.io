02 Research focus

Three questions I keep returning to

What is inside the model, what does it actually use, and when should we trust what it tells us about its own confidence?

I.

Mechanistic interpretability

Recovering human-meaningful concepts with sparse autoencoders, then testing which of them a downstream classifier causally relies on rather than merely encodes.

→ PATH-DISSECT, MENTIS

II.

Alignment & preference optimization

Training lightweight LoRA adapters for helpfulness, harmlessness and honesty with DPO, then composing them at decoding time to steer behaviour without retraining the base model.

→ Decoding-time alignment, MENTIS

III.

Uncertainty & reliability

When confidence signals mislead: grammar-constrained decoding can flip the uncertainty ranking of inputs, which matters for anyone routing requests on entropy.

→ Constrained decoding & uncertainty

  1. Jun 2026 – Present
    Research Paper in preparation · UncertaiNLP 2026 @ EMNLP

    Constrained Decoding Can Reverse Which Inputs Look Uncertain

    LLM uncertainty research

    • Designed controlled experiments on Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct across 100 prompts using XGrammar-based JSON constraints.
    • Comparing pre- and post-mask token entropy revealed a reversal in uncertainty ranking: AUROC moved from 0.125–0.175 before masking to 0.613–0.750 after, so decisive outputs can be made to look uncertain.
    • Showed the effect depends on the distribution of surviving probability mass rather than how much is removed, and proposed pre-mask entropy and surviving probability as more reliable routing signals.
    LLMsUncertaintyXGrammarEntropy
