May – Aug 2026

Constrained Decoding & LLM Uncertainty

Independent research · sole-author paper, accepted at UncertaiNLP @ EMNLP 2026

  • Custom decoding loop capturing logits before and after XGrammar JSON masking on Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct across 100 controlled prompts.
  • Masking reverses which inputs look uncertain: ranking AUROC shifts from 0.13–0.18 to 0.61–0.75 while post-mask entropy still looks normal.
  • The reversal tracks how surviving probability mass is distributed, not how much is removed — pre-mask entropy is the reliable signal.

Swiss-Knife & MENTIS

Alignment research · Pragya Lab, BITS Pilani – Goa · both submitted to AAAI 2027

  • Swiss-Knife: steers a frozen Qwen2.5-7B with hot-swappable helpful, honest and harmless DPO-LoRA adapters, switching objectives in 0.05 ms without retraining.
  • Harmonic F1 0.797 vs. 0.750 over 5 decode-time baselines, lowest refusal rate and 47% lower toxicity.
  • MENTIS: built hidden-state extraction, torsion metrics and CKA, RepE and cosine baselines across 4 aligned 7–8B model pairs; torsion separated safe from unsafe prompts at AUC 0.89 vs. 0.64.
