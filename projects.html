Constrained Decoding & LLM Uncertainty

LLM uncertainty research · paper in preparation, UncertaiNLP 2026 (EMNLP workshop)

  • Controlled experiments on Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct across 100 prompts with XGrammar-based JSON constraints.
  • Post-mask entropy reverses the uncertainty ranking: AUROC 0.125–0.175 before masking, 0.613–0.750 after.
  • The effect tracks the distribution of surviving probability mass, not the amount removed.
