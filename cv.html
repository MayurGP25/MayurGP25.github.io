Academic and research projects

Constrained Decoding Can Reverse Which Inputs Look Uncertain

May – Aug 2026

Independent research project · Bengaluru, India

  • Built a custom decoding loop that captures logits before and after XGrammar JSON masking on Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct across 100 controlled prompts.
  • Discovered that grammar masking reverses which inputs look uncertain, shifting ranking AUROC from 0.13–0.18 to 0.61–0.75 while post-mask entropy still looks normal, so confidence monitors receive no warning.
  • Demonstrated that the reversal depends on how surviving probability mass is distributed rather than how much is removed, establishing pre-mask entropy as the reliable signal; sole-author paper accepted at UncertaiNLP @ EMNLP 2026.
